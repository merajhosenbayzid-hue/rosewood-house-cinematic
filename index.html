The Property

One House, Seen Properly

Every feature below is taken only from what the ten photographs actually show — a modern two-storey house in warm red brick and charcoal-grey stone, with dark teak woodwork, a steep dark-grey pitched roof and large warm-lit glass windows, standing among frangipani and rain trees. No price, location, size or review has been invented, because none exists for this design concept.

Exterior Front

Warm red brick over a charcoal-grey stone ground floor, dark teak panelling and window frames, and a steep dark-grey pitched roof — with lawns, flowering frangipani and tall rain trees in front.

Gate & Driveway

Heavy dark-teak gates open onto a herringbone brick driveway that curves up to the front door, shaded by a flowering frangipani.

Entrance Foyer

An oversized teak pivot door, a dark stone floor and a teak staircase with black railings, lit by warm globe pendants.

Living Room

Exposed brick walls, a beamed teak ceiling and a charcoal stone fireplace, with wide doors opening straight onto the garden.

Dining Room

A long teak dining table beneath glass pendant lights, in a teak-panelled room whose openings frame the garden on both sides.

Kitchen

Full-height teak cabinetry, an exposed brick backsplash and a charcoal stone island with woven stools, plus built-in ovens and folding glass doors to the garden.

Master Bedroom

A vaulted teak ceiling over brick and teak-panelled walls, with glazed doors opening to the garden on both sides of the bed.

Swimming Pool

A rectangular pool edged in charcoal stone and dark teak decking, lined with sun loungers and facing the warm-lit rear of the house.

Garden Terrace

A broad teak deck with deep outdoor sofas, set in planted gardens of frangipani and rain trees behind the house.

By Night

Seen from above after dark — glowing windows, the lit pool behind the house, the brick drive and garden lights scattered through the trees.

About This Experience

This website is a photo-based cinematic journey. It presents ten photographs of one consistent house — The Rosewood House — as a smooth, scroll-controlled film. It is not a true 3D reconstruction: no 3D model of the house exists behind these images, and the spaces between photographs are bridged by camera-style motion (slow zoom, pan and crossfade), not by real geometry.

About the photographs: The Rosewood House is a design concept house. The ten images on this page are an AI-generated photo set created for this presentation — they are not photographs of a built or listed property, and nothing on this page should be read as an offer of a real house for sale or rent.
